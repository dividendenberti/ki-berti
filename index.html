About Robert Bartonek

An industry career built on knowing what sells.

For more than three decades, I have been working at the heart of the international DRTV, teleshopping, and infomercial industry. My career began in the early days of direct response television, and over the years I have helped shape, produce, and launch countless successful TV shows, commercials, and product campaigns across Europe and worldwide.

My core expertise lies in developing market-ready product concepts, producing high-impact infomercials, and building scalable sales strategies for TV, online, and retail. I work closely with manufacturers, innovators, and distributors to transform strong ideas into profitable consumer products.

Throughout my career, I have collaborated with leading teleshopping networks, production companies, and global partners. My work includes product launches, brand building, script development, creative direction, and international distribution.

Today, I support companies in bringing their products to market with the same passion as on day one — combining experience, intuition, and a deep understanding of what truly sells.
